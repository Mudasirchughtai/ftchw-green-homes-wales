PROJECT: FIRST TIME CENTRAL HEATING WALES — GREEN HOMES WALES LANDING PAGE

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Build a premium, high-converting landing page that helps Welsh homeowners check their potential eligibility for:

- The Boiler Upgrade Scheme
- Up to £9,000 towards an eligible heat pump
- Green Homes Wales interest-free funding from £1,000 to £25,000
- Fully funded retrofit assessment and coordination support
- Air-source and ground-source heat pumps
- Related energy-efficiency improvements where appropriate

All completed leads must enter Privyr CRM instantly with every qualification answer, source, campaign and consent record.

IMPORTANT FUNDING WORDING

The £9,000 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant is not available to every homeowner.

Use the following accurate wording:

"Eligible homeowners may receive £7,500 towards an air-source or ground-source heat pump. From 21 July 2026, eligible off-gas-grid homes replacing oil or LPG heating may qualify for an increased grant of £9,000."

Before 21 July 2026, the page must say:

"Up to £9,000 available from 21 July 2026 for eligible off-gas-grid homes."

From 21 July 2026, the page can say:

"Check whether your home could qualify for up to £9,000 towards an eligible heat pump."

The enhanced £9,000 grant currently applies to qualifying off-gas-grid homes replacing oil or LPG. Other qualifying properties may receive £7,500.

The £9,000 claim must be controlled through a central configuration setting so it can be updated or removed when scheme rules or dates change.

GREEN HOMES WALES WORDING

Use:

"Interest-free Green Homes Wales funding from £1,000 to £25,000, subject to status, affordability, credit checks, scheme criteria and final approval."

Also use:

"Fully funded expert retrofit assessment and coordination support may be available through Green Homes Wales."

Do not advertise a guaranteed £1,000 assessment grant unless written confirmation is supplied.

Do not describe the website form as a formal finance application.

FAQ SECTION

Include the following questions.

1. Is the £9,000 heat-pump grant available to everyone?

"No. The enhanced £9,000 grant applies to eligible off-gas-grid homes replacing oil or LPG during the applicable scheme period. Other qualifying heat-pump installations may receive £7,500."

2. Do I need to repay the Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant?

"The Boiler Upgrade Scheme payment is a grant rather than a loan. The installer normally applies for it and deducts it from the eligible installation cost."

3. Can Green Homes Wales be used with the Boiler Upgrade Scheme?

"In some circumstances, Green Homes Wales funding may be used alongside the Boiler Upgrade Scheme to help cover qualifying costs remaining after the grant, subject to the applicable scheme rules."

4. How much can I apply for through Green Homes Wales?

"Eligible applicants may apply for interest-free funding from £1,000 to £25,000, subject to affordability, credit checks, scheme criteria and final approval."

5. Is Green Homes Wales means-tested?

"The scheme is not currently described as means-tested, although applicants must pass affordability and credit assessments."

6. Do I need to be receiving benefits?

"No. Receiving benefits is not a general Green Homes Wales requirement, although other home-energy schemes may have different criteria."

7. Who applies for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant?

"The chosen MCS-certified installer normally applies on the homeowner's behalf."

8. Does completing this form guarantee funding?

"No. The form provides an initial indication only. Formal eligibility depends on the applicant, property, installer assessment, scheme criteria, credit status where finance is involved and available funding."

9. Is First Time Central Heating Wales a government organisation?

"No. First Time Central Heating Wales is an independent marketing and installer-introduction service."

10. Does First Time Central Heating Wales carry out the installation?

"No. Installation work is completed by an independent installer holding the appropriate accreditations and registrations."

11. Will I have to proceed after completing the eligibility check?

"No. Completing the form does not commit you to an installer, loan or installation contract."
